🎄 Christmas Lists by Nick

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Christmas Lists by Nick (giftsbynick.netlify.app) is a small, private app for sharing Christmas wish lists with family. It is run by Nick, for family and friends. This page explains what the app keeps and why.

What the app stores

  • Your account: your name, your email address, and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password if you made one. Passwords are never stored in plain text.
  • If you sign in with Google: Google shares only your name, email address, and a Google account ID with the app. The app never sees your Google password and does not access anything else in your Google account.
  • Contact info you choose to add: phone numbers and emails on your profile, so people in your circles can reach you.
  • What you put in the app: your circles, wish lists, items, links, photos you upload, which items you opened, and which gifts you checked off.

Who can see it

  • Your lists and contact info are visible only to people in the circles you share them with.
  • When you check off a gift, the app records it privately. Other people only see that the item is “Taken,” never your name, and the person whose list it is sees nothing.
  • Nick, as the app’s admin, can see each account’s name and email in order to help reset forgotten passwords.

What the app does not do

  • No ads, no tracking, and no selling or sharing your information with anyone.
  • Information from Google sign-in is used only to log you in.

Where it is kept

Everything is stored with Netlify, the service that hosts the website. A login cookie keeps you signed in on your device for up to 90 days, or until you log out.

Deleting your information

You can delete your own lists and items at any time inside the app. To have your whole account removed, ask Nick and he will delete it.

Questions

Ask Nick directly. This app is just for family and friends.
